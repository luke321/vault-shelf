# 0031 - Picker and reader controls

The fourteen colour swatches occupy two rows of seven in every picker. A sixth binding,
Minimal, follows Original: plain leather, no bands or frame, and a straight ribbon. Its
height is 131px; automatic binding selection includes all six styles.

Default contents order is two buttons, A-Z and Date, with a pressed state showing the
effective value. Untouched creation drafts inherit; pressing a button saves that mode.
The edge index has a fixed 56px width in both modes, compresses when long (design/0032), and has 72px of
right-page clearance. Changing mode preserves the note and scroll position.

The root grid gives the library rail its own row above both library and reader. Search stays
on the left, the vault title is centred and actions sit right; narrow layouts put the title
on a separate centred row. Opening a book never hides the search field or query. The old
oldest/newest button is removed; saved ordering values still load for compatibility.

Reader marks carry their source binding and share its ribbon cut, including the insertion
stub. Vellum uses the same diagonal cut and linen texture in both places, with a 4px-wide
spine ribbon. Label padding keeps text above the cut ends.

Manage says fourteen colours. Its colour rules have their own wrapping row and a 160px
select so year/decade controls cannot push actions outside the sheet. Selecting a rule
turns off per-book colour variation. Pick shelves offer no variation toggle, including in
the builder, and ignore old variation flags so references keep their source's dye.

The targeted checks measure index width, stable rail bounds, query visibility, six matching
ribbon cuts, palette rows and Manage bounds at desktop and 390px. The automatic binding
distribution changes the expected spine heights in the golden snapshot; membership, ten
rows, 227 spines and 52 plaques remain unchanged in its unseeded 4,938-note fixture.
